Do not run any retrieval strategy, do not consult BM25 or embedding rankings, and do not drop or rewrite a query because a strategy would get it wrong. Validate frequently with `npx tsc -p tsconfig.eval.json` plus a node one-liner calling resolveDataset on the compiled module for just these resumes without minimums, and fix only resolution/overlap errors.
