<objective>
Build the dataset foundation for retrieval evaluation: types, a loader that chunks synthetic resumes with the app's real `chunkResume` and resolves stable-key relevance labels to chunk ids with loud validation, three synthetic resumes, and a chunk dump CLI for labelling.

Purpose: `chunkResume` assigns ids with `crypto.randomUUID()` (see `newId()` in lib/vault/chunker.ts), so ids change on every load. Labels therefore must point at chunks by `kind` + a distinctive text fragment and be resolved at load time (locked decision). This plan makes that resolution trustworthy before any queries are written.
Output: dataset types, loader/validator, RESUMES, empty QUERIES export (populated by plan 01-02), dump CLI, validator tests.
</objective>

- Behaviour to know when writing resumes: section headings matched by SECTION_PATTERNS (SUMMARY, EXPERIENCE, PROJECTS, EDUCATION, SKILLS, ACHIEVEMENTS/CERTIFICATIONS...; REFERENCES/INTERESTS/LANGUAGES are skipped); inside EXPERIENCE/PROJECTS each role header line (contains a year/"Present" or " at ", " | ", ", ") starts a block; each bullet line (•, -, *, 1.) becomes a `bullet` chunk whose title is the joined role header; the role block also becomes one `experience`/`project` container chunk whose text contains the header plus every bullet prefixed "- "; summary keeps only lines with more than 6 words; each ACHIEVEMENTS line is its own `win` chunk titled "Achievement"; SKILLS and EDUCATION become one chunk each.

From lib/retrieval/bm25.ts:
- `export function tokenize(text: string): string[]` — lowercases, strips punctuation except + # . -, drops stopwords and tokens of length 1, applies crude suffix stemming. This is exactly what BM25 sees.

    - A query with no label of grade >= 2, a grade outside 1..3, a duplicate query id, or an unknown resumeId throws.
    - All problems are collected and reported together in one DatasetError (message lists every problem), not just the first.
    - With minimums enabled, fewer than 3 resumes, fewer than 60 queries, or any resume with fewer than 25 chunks throws.
